ZeltaDrop

Privacy Policy

Clear, transparent information about how ZeltaDrop handles data, local file transfers, device permissions, advertising, and privacy choices.

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

ZeltaDrop is developed by ZeltaBits Labs.

ZeltaDrop is designed for fast, private, local file transfers between compatible nearby devices. The core transfer experience does not require a ZeltaDrop account or ZeltaDrop-operated cloud storage for your files.

Privacy at a glance: ZeltaDrop is built around direct local transfers. Your transferred files are not uploaded to a ZeltaDrop cloud storage service as part of the core transfer feature.

1. Information We Handle

ZeltaDrop is designed to minimize information required for local file transfer. Depending on the features you use, the application may process information locally on your device such as:

  • Selected file names, sizes, and transfer metadata.
  • Device name used for local pairing or display.
  • Nearby-device and local network information needed for transfer.
  • QR pairing/session information when you use QR pairing.
  • Transfer history stored by the application.
  • Camera input when you choose to scan a QR code.

ZeltaDrop does not require an account for normal local transfers.

2. Device Permissions

Camera

Camera access is used for QR-code scanning when you choose that feature. It is not intended for unrelated purposes.

Nearby Devices / Wi-Fi

Android nearby-device and Wi-Fi permissions may be required to discover or communicate with a nearby device for local file transfer.

Network Access

Network access may be used for local transfer communication and by supporting services such as advertising and consent management.

3. Local File Transfers

When you select files to send, ZeltaDrop processes those files to establish and perform the transfer you requested.

The core transfer design is intended to move files directly between nearby devices rather than storing them on a ZeltaDrop cloud service.

Use with trusted devices: You should verify the receiving device before accepting a transfer and only send files to people or devices you trust.

Depending on the production version and enabled security configuration, transfer sessions may use authenticated pairing, encrypted transport, integrity verification, and temporary session information.

4. Advertising

ZeltaDrop may display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads, including Google AdMob.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and advertising configuration, advertising services may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, and ad interaction information.

ZeltaDrop does not sell your personal files or transfer content to advertisers.

5. Consent & Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, ZeltaDrop may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or other consent mechanisms to provide information about advertising and privacy choices.

Available choices can vary by region and applicable legal requirements. If a privacy-options control is available in the app, you can use it to review or change applicable choices.

6. File Storage

Files received through ZeltaDrop are stored on the receiving device according to the application's storage implementation and Android storage rules.

ZeltaDrop may temporarily create partial files during an active transfer. These temporary files are intended to be removed if a transfer fails, is cancelled, or does not complete successfully.

You remain responsible for files stored on your device, including deleting files you no longer need.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect local transfers and application data. Depending on the current production implementation, these may include:

  • Secure QR-based pairing and short-lived sessions.
  • One-time authentication/session controls.
  • Encrypted transfer data.
  • SHA-256 integrity verification.
  • Temporary partial-file handling.
  • Filename/path validation and transfer-size limits.

No software or network communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and Android version updated and use ZeltaDrop with devices and networks you trust.

8. Sharing of Information

ZeltaDrop does not sell your personal information or transferred files.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when needed for features such as advertising, consent management, diagnostics, or other explicitly enabled functionality. Such providers process information according to their own policies and applicable law.

The core purpose of ZeltaDrop is not to upload your personal files to a ZeltaDrop-operated cloud service.

9. Data Retention

ZeltaDrop does not intentionally retain transferred files on ZeltaBits Labs servers as part of the core local-transfer functionality.

Local transfer history or application information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application data, or remove the application according to Android behavior.

Third-party advertising and consent providers may retain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

ZeltaDrop is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or providing personalized services.

If you believe a child has provided information that requires action, please contact us using the details below.

11. Third-Party Services

ZeltaDrop may use third-party services that support advertising, consent management, application distribution, diagnostics, or other app functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in ZeltaDrop, applicable laws, third-party services, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this page will be updated.
