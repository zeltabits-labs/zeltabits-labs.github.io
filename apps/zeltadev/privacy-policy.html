ZeltaDev Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

ZeltaDev is a developer utility application developed by ZeltaBits Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ZeltaDev Android application.

1. Information We Collect

ZeltaDev does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address through the core functionality of the application.

Most developer utilities provided by ZeltaDev are designed to process the information you enter locally on your device.

2. Local Data

ZeltaDev may store certain application data locally on your device, such as History and Favorites, when these features are used.

This locally stored information is intended to remain on your device and is not intentionally uploaded to servers operated by ZeltaBits Labs as part of the core application functionality.

You can remove locally stored application data by using the available settings in the application or by clearing the application's data through your Android device settings.

3. Advertising

ZeltaDev may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, application information, and interactions with advertisements, subject to Google's applicable policies and your device or advertising settings.

Advertising data may be processed by Google and other advertising partners in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Internet Access

ZeltaDev may use an Internet connection for advertising and other network-related functionality.

The core developer utilities are designed to operate locally and do not require an online account.

5. Third-Party Services

ZeltaDev may use third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads, to provide advertising functionality.

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's Privacy

ZeltaDev is a general-purpose developer utility application and is not specifically directed toward children.

ZeltaBits Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by ZeltaDev remains on your device until you remove it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

ZeltaBits Labs does not maintain a user account database for the core ZeltaDev application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, legal requirements, or our practices.

When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
