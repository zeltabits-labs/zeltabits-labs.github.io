Privacy Policy

Clear information about how ZeltaScan handles permissions, local data, advertising, consent, and third-party services.

Effective: September 9, 2026 Developer: Zeltabits Labs

ZeltaScan is a QR Code and Barcode Scanner and Generator app operated by Zeltabits Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use ZeltaScan.

Privacy at a glance: ZeltaScan uses your camera for scanning, can process images you select for scanning, and can keep scan history and saved items locally on your device. ZeltaScan also uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.

1. Camera Permission

ZeltaScan requests camera permission when you use the scanner. Camera access is used to detect and scan QR codes and supported barcodes.

ZeltaScan does not intentionally upload camera frames to Zeltabits Labs' own servers for storage.

2. Photos and Images

When you choose an image for QR or barcode scanning, ZeltaScan processes the image you select for that scanning operation.

Selected images are not intentionally uploaded to Zeltabits Labs' own servers for storage.

3. Scan History and Saved Items

Scan results, generated-code information, favorites, and related history may be stored locally on your device so you can view, copy, share, or manage them later.

ZeltaScan does not intentionally upload this locally stored history or saved-item information to Zeltabits Labs' own servers.

You can delete locally stored history and saved items using the controls provided in the app.

4. QR Code and Barcode Content

Content scanned or generated through ZeltaScan may contain:

  • Text
  • Website URLs
  • Wi-Fi information
  • Contact information
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Location information
  • UPI or payment-related information
  • Other information encoded in QR codes or barcodes

ZeltaScan processes this content to provide the scanning, generating, viewing, copying, saving, and sharing features requested by the user.

You are responsible for information you choose to scan, generate, save, copy, or share.

5. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

ZeltaScan uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, advertising request, user choices, location, and applicable requirements, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or receive certain information.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App and user interaction information
  • Advertising-related information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information related to fraud prevention, security, and ad delivery

This information may be used by Google and its advertising services for purposes such as advertising, analytics, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and improving advertising services.

Advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited depending on user choices, regional requirements, consent status, and available ad-serving settings.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law or regulation, ZeltaScan uses Google's consent mechanisms to obtain and respect users' choices regarding advertising and related data processing.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be presented with choices regarding personalized advertising and related data processing.

You can also manage available advertising and privacy settings through Android and Google settings.

7. Internet Connection and Third-Party Services

ZeltaScan may use an internet connection for advertising, application update-related services, and actions that intentionally open or share online content.

ZeltaScan may interact with third-party services, including Google services. When you interact with a third-party service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Zeltabits Labs does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by ZeltaScan. Information stored locally by the app remains on your device unless you choose to copy, share, or otherwise transmit it.

Third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads apply their own security and data protection practices. Information handled by supported Google Mobile Ads services is transmitted using appropriate security measures such as encryption in transit.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

ZeltaScan is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed toward children.

Zeltabits Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Zeltabits Labs, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. Your Choices and Controls

  • Deny camera permission when scanning is not needed.
  • Choose which image to provide for image-based scanning.
  • Delete locally stored scan history and saved items.
  • Manage available advertising and privacy settings through Android and Google settings.
  • Make applicable advertising consent choices when Google's consent mechanism is presented.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, advertising services, third-party services, data practices, or applicable legal and regulatory requirements change.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
