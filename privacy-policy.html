Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy describes how ZeltaBits Labs handles information when you visit this website or contact us about software-development services. Individual applications or third-party services may have their own privacy notices.

1. Information you provide

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, name if provided, message, project requirements, files or other information you choose to share. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers or banking credentials.

2. Website technical information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the service. Please review GitHub's own privacy information for details of its processing.

3. How information is used

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, discuss and deliver agreed projects, provide support, maintain security, keep appropriate business records and meet legal obligations.

4. Payments

If you purchase a service and a payment provider is used, payment details are processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. We may receive transaction references, payment status and limited information needed to reconcile a payment. We do not ask you to email full card details or banking credentials. Payments are accepted only for ZeltaBits Labs' own agreed services, not on behalf of unrelated clients or third parties.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to host the website, communicate with you, process an agreed payment, deliver a project or comply with law. Providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

6. Retention and security

We retain correspondence and project/payment records for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including legitimate business and legal recordkeeping. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. We may need to verify your request before acting.

8. Children and external links

This website is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. External links are provided for convenience; third-party sites operate under their own policies.

We may update this policy by publishing a revised version on this page. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
